Approach

Every detail tells a story

Amirali designs interiors around the people who live in them and the pieces they love. Architecture sets the structure. Decoration, art and carefully chosen objects give each room its voice.

From the first sketch to the final styling, the work is guided by color, material and close attention to detail.

Styling

The objects make the room

Glass, porcelain, crystal and sculptural lighting are placed with the same care as the architecture, so that every corner reads as a composition.

Home staging and accessories complete a project and prepare a property to be seen at its best.

About

Amirali Akhlaghi

Amirali is an interior architect and decorator with a B.A. in Interior Architecture. His practice moves between architecture and styling, from the plan of a space to the objects that finish it.

He designs residential interiors, most recently a new home in Tehran, developing the concept, the design and the final styling. Home staging and accessories complete the work.

He works between Turkey, Iran and the USA, with projects worldwide. Clients can write to him directly on Instagram or WhatsApp.
