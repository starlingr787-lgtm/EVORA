A bathroom is the smallest room you’ll spend the most mornings in. We treat it that way. Every tile laid to a line, every fitting chosen to outlast the trend.

Our story

Technique began in Glasgow in 1999 and has stayed a family business ever since. The people who walk through your home and draw up the plan are the same people who arrive on the first morning, lay the last tile and leave the room spotless.

Over the years that has meant a lot of bathrooms across the city and the West of Scotland, from small cloakrooms to full marble suites. Each one begins with a conversation, a tape measure and a cup of tea, and ends only when it feels like it was always meant to be there.
